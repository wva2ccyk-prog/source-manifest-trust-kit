# Risk Policy

Risk labels are deterministic triage labels, not truth labels.

## Important boundaries

- Low risk means the claim can be restated only inside its supplied source boundary.
- Medium risk means review is required before downstream reliance.
- High and critical risk claims remain unresolved unless independent primary evidence is supplied outside this runtime.
- Social, community, private, deleted, or rumor-like sources must not be promoted to official or verified status by repetition.
- Finance advice, target-price, allocation, probability, expected-return, or trade-action wording is masked or excluded from report surfaces.

## Not provided

The tool does not produce investment recommendations, buy/sell/hold calls, rankings, target prices, return forecasts, probability estimates, or market-timing conclusions.
